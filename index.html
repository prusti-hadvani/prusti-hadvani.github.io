#About

I'm a full-stack software engineer at Midaas Telesoft, working in the telemetry/IoT domain. Most of my day-to-day is Go — building REST APIs, real-time pipelines over WebSockets and MQTT, and securing service-to-service communication with JWT and X.509 certificates.
