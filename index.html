live pipeline demo

This is what I build at work, in miniature. Simulated devices publish readings, a broker fans them out to observers, and the dashboard updates the moment data arrives. It all runs in your browser, with no real backend.

devices→MQTT broker→Go service (auth · validate)→WebSocket / SSE→dashboard

how I structure services

Onion architecture

Domain rules sit in the center and know nothing about HTTP, MQTT, or the database. Use cases wrap the domain; adapters (REST handlers, MQTT clients, repositories) sit on the outside. Swapping a transport or a store doesn't touch business logic.

Observer for real-time

Incoming readings are published once and consumed by independent observers: persistence, WebSocket broadcast, alert rules. Adding a new consumer never means editing the ingestion path.

Secure by default

JWT access and refresh tokens for users, X.509 certificates for service-to-service trust, and validation at the edge of every adapter.

Tested seams

Because dependencies point inward, use cases are tested with in-memory repositories and fake publishers. Unit and integration tests run fast and catch regressions before release.
